About the clinic

Shree Shraddha Physiotherapy Clinic

Established in 2005, Shree Shraddha Physiotherapy Clinic has been a part of the Kolsewadi neighbourhood in Kalyan East for close to two decades, offering physiotherapy and rehabilitation care built around each patient's specific needs.

The clinic's work spans orthopedic physiotherapy, neurological rehabilitation and pediatric physiotherapy — with a patient-focused approach that pairs hands-on treatment with guided exercise and long-term recovery planning.

Dr. Rashmi Manohar Leads care at Shree Shraddha, with a focus on orthopedic, neurological and pediatric physiotherapy.

What we treat

Physiotherapy & rehabilitation services

A focused range of physiotherapy care, tailored to each patient's condition and recovery goals.

Orthopedic Physiotherapy

Care for joint, bone and muscle conditions, aimed at restoring movement and easing day-to-day discomfort.

Neurological Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation support for neurological conditions, structured around each patient's functional needs.

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Early-intervention physiotherapy for children, including developmental and movement-related conditions.

Neck & Back Pain Care

Assessment-led treatment for cervical and lumbar spine discomfort, aimed at steady, sustainable relief.

Knee & Shoulder Rehabilitation

Targeted rehabilitation programs to help rebuild strength and mobility in the knee and shoulder joints.

Arthritis Management

Ongoing physiotherapy support to help manage arthritis-related stiffness and joint discomfort.

Frozen Shoulder Rehabilitation

Structured mobility and strengthening work to gradually improve frozen shoulder range of motion.

Musculoskeletal & Nerve Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation for nerve and muscle disorders, focused on function, comfort and gradual progress.
